01 · Fastest daily capture

One-Click Job Clipper and Paste Autofill

Save openings from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, LinkedIn, and Simplify in a single click. Paste any job URL or posting snippet to fill company, role, location, source, and technical skills automatically. Spend your energy tailoring resumes instead of copying fields by hand.

  • Auto-detects ATS & job board URLs
  • Extracts skills & location instantaneously
02 · Visual stage tracking

Kanban Pipeline and Dated Stage History

Move applications across Saved, Applied, Interview, Offer, and Rejected columns as your search progresses. Every stage change is stamped automatically on a dated timeline so you always know when you applied and when a recruiter replied. Filter by company, skill, or stage in real time on desktop or phone.

  • 5 color-vision-safe pipeline stages
  • Automatic dated move history
03 · Zero switching cost

Universal Spreadsheet and Backup Import

Bring your existing tracker from Google Sheets, Notion, Simplify, Excel, or WaterlooWorks without retyping a single row. Smart header detection maps columns, normalizes stages, and validates links automatically before anything is added. Full JSON and CSV exports ensure your records are always portable.

  • RFC 4180 CSV, TSV & JSON support
  • Lossless round-trip export & undo
04 · Walk in prepared

Role-Matched Interview Prep and Timed Mock Sessions

Practice from a curated bank of 62 behavioral and technical questions matched to each application's role and stage. Run timed mock interviews with live STAR structure checks, filler-word detection, and speaking-pace feedback. Review your score trends and weakest areas before walking into the real call.

  • 62 role-aware practice questions
  • Live STAR & pacing feedback
05 · Never miss a deadline

Deadline Radar and Calendar Reminders

See overdue items, upcoming application deadlines, and quiet applications that need a follow-up nudge on your Today page. Export a standard calendar file (.ics) to put every deadline and interview onto Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook with two-stage alerts. Generate a self-addressed weekly email digest in one click.

  • One-click .ics calendar alerts
  • 7-day quiet application follow-up nudges
06 · Private by design

Offline-First Notebook and Search Analytics

Your application records live locally in your browser with an automatic draft retry queue that works on subway commutes and campus Wi-Fi. Spot patterns across your search with response-rate funnels, weekly application rhythm charts, and top employer skill bars. Optional end-to-end user-owned cloud sync keeps your devices in step when you choose.

  • 100% offline-capable PWA storage
  • Response funnel & skill frequency charts
