Delete your account

You can permanently delete your cloud account and cloud notebook from inside the web app.

  1. Open Settings and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Under Account & cloud sync, choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm your identity and the permanent deletion.

This deletes the Firebase Authentication account and the Firestore notebook stored under its user ID. It does not erase notebooks stored locally in your browsers; clear this site's browser data on each device to remove those copies.

If you cannot sign in, use Forgot password? in Settings first. For another deletion problem, open an issue in the project issue tracker without including passwords or notebook contents.
